# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

German-speaking visitors in and around Fürth, especially migrants and bilingual families, who need approachable help with everyday German, appointments, translation, integration, education, or community activities.

## Product Purpose

Present Irina Shevtsova as a trusted personal guide between languages and cultures, explain her services and current activities, build confidence through real photographs and testimonials, and make direct contact easy. For this migration, success means faithful visual and behavioral parity with the existing irinashevtsova.de while retaining the benefits of a lean static Next.js implementation.

## Brand Personality

Warm, encouraging, and practical. The voice is personal and reassuring rather than institutional; the experience should feel open, human, and quietly optimistic.

## Anti-references

- A generic consultant, agency, SaaS, or corporate-integration template.
- A redesign that replaces the established cream, green, orange, Courgette, Open Sans, photography, or generous pacing without explicit approval.
- Decorative effects, animation, or component abstractions that make the migration less faithful to the original WordPress site.
- Improvements that trade away clarity, accessibility, responsive behavior, or reduced-motion support.

## Design Principles

- Treat the original live site and its WordPress source as the visual and behavioral authority.
- Lead with Irina's human presence, lived experience, and real community imagery.
- Make every service and contact path understandable without specialist language.
- Preserve the calm, spacious cadence and familiar green/orange identity across viewports.
- Match motion intentionally while ensuring content remains available with reduced motion or failed scripts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, with semantic landmarks and headings, keyboard-visible controls, descriptive text alternatives where images convey meaning, adequate contrast, 44px touch targets where practical, and a complete prefers-reduced-motion path for every animation.
